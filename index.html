Set up your camera

  1. Draw your piano keyboard on a sheet of paper, flat on a desk.
  2. Prop your phone up against a cup, book, or stand so the camera points down at the paper.
  3. Make sure the whole keyboard is visible in frame, with good lighting.
  4. Tap Start below, then use Calibrate or Adjust Keys to match the zones to your drawing.
